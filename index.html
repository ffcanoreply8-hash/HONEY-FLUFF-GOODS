GOOD TO KNOW

A few sweet
details.

Can I order online yet?

You can explore the proposed menu and build a basket. Checkout currently prepares an email enquiry for you to review and send. It does not place a confirmed order or take payment. Availability and final pricing must be confirmed directly.

What do the prices and sizes mean?

Prices are suggested launch prices in Canadian dollars. Sizes and packaging are proposed. Final prices, sizes, applicable taxes, and any delivery fee will be confirmed before an order is accepted.

How do pickup and delivery work?

We’re exploring local pickup and delivery in Calgary. Location, delivery zones, fees, minimum orders, and preparation times are still being finalized. Contact us before planning a pickup or delivery.

Where can I find ingredients and allergens?

Recipes and supplier labels are not finalized. We cannot yet provide verified ingredient lists or allergen declarations. Do not rely on flavour names or illustrations to judge suitability. Ask us for confirmed information before ordering, particularly if you have an allergy.

How should I store my treats?

Keep ice cream frozen and chocolate away from heat. Follow the finished product label for exact temperatures, shelf life, refrigeration, and instructions after opening. Proposed storage notes in product details are general guidance.

What payment methods will you accept?

Online payments are not enabled on this preview. Available payment methods will be confirmed directly. Do not send card details through email or the enquiry form.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Contact us as soon as possible. Cancellation windows, refunds, and policies for perishable goods will be shared before confirmed ordering opens. This preview does not establish a final refund policy.

Do you offer event or larger orders?

Tell us your occasion, approximate quantity, and preferred date by phone or email. We’ll discuss what may be possible; submitting an enquiry does not reserve a date.

How do saved favourites and the basket work?

Favourites and your basket are saved only in this browser when storage is available. There is no account, and saving an item does not reserve stock or place an order.

How do launch updates work?

The launch form prepares an email request in your email app. You must review and send it yourself. Your address is not automatically added to a mailing list. You can ask to stop updates at any time.
