GOOD TO KNOW

Less wondering.
More sweetness.

How does ordering work?

Explore the menu, build a bag, and prepare an email enquiry. Review and send it from your email app. Your order is confirmed only after we agree on availability, final pricing and arrangements. No payment is taken here.

Are these the final prices and pack sizes?

This is a launch collection preview. Prices are estimates in Canadian dollars and pack sizes are proposed. Packaging artwork may show earlier sizes. We’ll confirm final sizes, taxes, delivery and total price directly.

Where are ingredients and allergen details?

Final recipes and verified ingredient and allergen details are not yet available. Please request confirmed information before ordering. Flavour names and artwork do not establish suitability.

Can I arrange pickup, delivery or a larger order?

We’re planning local pickup and delivery in Calgary. Ask about your location, preferred date or event. Dates, fees, order minimums and service areas require confirmation.

How do favourites and my bag work?

They are saved in this browser, where storage is available. There is no account, payment or stock reservation. Clearing browser data removes saved items.

What about storage, changes and cancellations?

Follow final product labels for storage and shelf life. Contact us about changes as early as possible. Applicable cancellation and refund terms will be confirmed before accepting an order.

Your information, simply.

Your bag, favourites and popup and sound preferences are stored in this browser. Order, feedback and launch forms prepare email drafts; they do not send automatically. Information you choose to email is used to respond to your request. Don’t include payment details or sensitive medical information.
